The operating principle

Automation should leave room for judgment.

01 / GROUNDING

Start with the rule.

Use maintained policy and product information as the basis for decisions before suggesting an answer.

02 / BOUNDARIES

Decide what can be sent.

Deterministic rules reject risky promises and give escalations a defined route.

03 / CONTROL

Keep the operator informed.

Conversations, tickets, notes and manual actions remain available to the support team.
