# Returns and Warranty Policy

## Return Window and Process
- Channel: All Harbor & Pine retail products are sold exclusively through Amazon. Consequently, all product return authorizations and return shipping labels are managed directly by Amazon Customer Service.
- Return Window: Customers may return items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery.
- Starting a Return: Customers should log in to their Amazon account, navigate to "Your Orders", locate the Harbor & Pine item, select "Return or replace items", and follow the prompts to generate a prepaid drop-off label.
- System Limitation: Harbor & Pine customer support staff and automated systems cannot initiate or expedite Amazon return labels or refund transactions directly.

## Refund Inquiries and Disputes
- Amazon processes refunds directly back to the customer's original payment method once the carrier scans the return shipment.
- Any customer asking for an immediate cash refund, demanding money back, threatening chargebacks, or disputing an Amazon transaction must be escalated immediately to a human colleague.
- Never promise or quote specific refund deposit dates in automated responses.

## Limited Warranty Coverage
- Harbor & Pine provides a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects on all paddle boards and flat-pack furniture.
- Warranty covers seam delamination, valve defects, material failure under normal recreational use, and factory structural defects.
- Warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, punctures from sharp rocks or debris, commercial rental use, improper storage (such as outdoor weather exposure), or modifications.
