# Flat-Pack Shelving

## Shelving Models and Dimensions
Harbor & Pine produces four standard flat-pack modular shelving units:
- HP-S3: 3-shelf vertical bookcase. Dimensions: 106 cm high x 80 cm wide x 30 cm deep.
- HP-S5: 5-shelf vertical bookcase. Dimensions: 176 cm high x 80 cm wide x 30 cm deep.
- HP-C4: Cube storage unit 2x2 (4 compartments). Dimensions: 77 cm high x 77 cm wide x 39 cm deep.
- HP-C9: Cube storage unit 3x3 (9 compartments). Dimensions: 112 cm high x 112 cm wide x 39 cm deep.

All models are available in four laminate finishes: natural oak, white, black, and walnut. The model code and finish are printed on the product packaging label and on page 1 of the assembly instructions.

## Assembly Instructions and Tips
- Assemble all shelving units on a clean carpet, soft blanket, or the flattened cardboard shipping carton to protect panel edges from scratching.
- Back Panel Orientation: For HP-S3 and HP-S5 bookcases, the finished/colored laminate side of the thin rear panel must face inward toward the shelves. The raw unfinished fiberboard backing faces outward toward the wall.
- Anchoring: All units 106 cm and taller include a wall-mounting safety bracket kit. Always secure bookcases to wall studs to prevent tipping hazards.

## Missing Hardware and Screws
If any fasteners, cam locks, wooden dowels, shelf pins, or screws are missing from the packaging:
- S-series bookcases require Hardware Kit HK-S.
- C-series cube units require Hardware Kit HK-C.
Hardware kits are dispatched free of charge. Required details from customer: order number, shelving model code, and shipping address.

## Damaged Parts and Replacements
- 1 or 2 Damaged Panels: We ship individual replacement panels directly from our warehouse. Panel numbers are stamped on raw edges and referenced in the manual diagram (e.g., HP-S3-04 for panel 4 of model HP-S3). Required details: order number, model code, finish color, and panel number(s).
- 3 or More Damaged Panels or Damaged Outer Frame: We dispatch a complete new unit replacement.
- Wrong Finish or Color Delivered: Escalate to a human support agent for replacement exchange processing.
