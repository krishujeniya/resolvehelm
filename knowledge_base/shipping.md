# Shipping Policies

## Amazon Order Fulfillment
- Initial product purchases made on Amazon are fulfilled by Amazon logistics (FBA).
- Shipping carriers, delivery estimates, transit updates, and carrier rescheduling are controlled entirely by Amazon.
- Support agents cannot view real-time Amazon GPS courier data, reroute delivery trucks, or expedite in-transit Amazon parcels. Customers can track their parcels via "Your Orders" on Amazon.
- If an order is overdue, missing, or marked delivered without arrival, escalate to a human agent to verify details with Amazon logistics.

## Replacement Parts and Hardware Dispatch
- All replacement parts (replacement panels, hardware kits HK-S/HK-C, paddles, fins, pumps, patch kits) are shipped directly from Harbor & Pine fulfillment warehouses free of charge.
- Processing Time: Replacement orders leave the warehouse within 2 business days.
- Delivery Time: Standard transit takes 3 to 7 business days depending on destination.
- Tracking: An automated email with carrier tracking details is generated as soon as the dispatch label is created.
- Geographic Scope: We ship replacement parts to all addresses eligible for primary Amazon delivery within our operating regions.
