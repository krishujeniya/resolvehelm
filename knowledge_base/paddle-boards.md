# Inflatable Paddle Boards

## Sizing and Models
Harbor & Pine offers three sizes of inflatable stand-up paddle boards (SUP), designed for different rider weights and use cases:
- 10'6" All-round: For riders up to 85 kg (187 lbs). Ideal for beginners, youth, and calm lake or harbor paddling.
- 11' All-round: For riders up to 110 kg (242 lbs). Our most stable board, suitable for all skill levels and for carrying light cargo or a child/dog.
- 12'6" Touring: For riders up to 130 kg (286 lbs). Longer and narrower profile designed for faster cruising, long-distance touring, and experienced paddlers.

If a rider's weight falls between two sizes or if they plan to carry extra gear, always recommend choosing the larger board for better buoyancy and stability.

## Inflation and Pressure Guidelines
- Recommended operating pressure is 12 to 15 PSI.
- Below 12 PSI, the board lacks rigidity and will flex in the middle under rider weight.
- Do not exceed 15 PSI.
- Temperature changes affect internal pressure: losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight as ambient temperature cools down is completely normal thermal contraction and does not indicate a puncture or leak.

## Slow Leak Troubleshooting and Valve Check
Over 90% of reported slow leaks are caused by a loose valve core rather than a hole in the board. Customers reporting air loss must perform the valve check first:
1. Inflate the board to 15 PSI.
2. Mix mild dish soap with water and spray or brush the solution around the valve circumference. If bubbles form, air is escaping around the valve seat.
3. Deflate the board slightly, insert the black plastic valve wrench from the repair kit into the valve notches, and firmly turn clockwise one quarter turn until tight.
4. Re-inflate to 15 PSI and observe the pressure after 24 hours.

If the board still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours after performing the valve check:
- Within 30 days of delivery: A brand-new replacement board is provided.
- More than 30 days after delivery: A comprehensive patch kit with instructions is provided.
- Split seams along the rail or manufacturing defects: Must be escalated to a human team member.
